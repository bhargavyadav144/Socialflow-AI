### 🌟 What makes SocialFlow AI unique?

- **Retain**: Automatically extracts structured observations from multi-channel posts (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube).
- **Recall**: Semantic & temporal memory retrieval across 6 distinct categories (Audience, Content, Performance, Strategy, Profile, Conversation).
- **Reflect**: Synthesizes mental models to recommend high-probability content strategies backed by actual historical evidence.
