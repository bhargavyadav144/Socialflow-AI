🚀 Introducing SocialMind AI: The Social Media Strategy Agent That Remembers.

Why do most AI social media tools give generic advice like "Post more reels" or "Use trending music"? 

Because standard LLMs operate statelessly — they forget your past post performance, audience preferences, and previous decisions the moment a chat ends.

We built **SocialMind AI** to solve this.

Powered by **Vectorize Hindsight**, SocialMind AI gives your AI strategist long-term persistent memory:

🧠 **Remembers Content History:** Learns which topics (like Python vs broad promos) generate high engagement and saves.
📊 **Analyzes Audience Demographics:** Tailors recommendations based on real past comments and student audience feedback.
⚡ **Continuous Strategic Improvement:** Automatically recalls past wins and failures before giving new recommendations.

Key Architecture:
• Frontend: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Recharts
• Backend: Python FastAPI, SQLAlchemy
• Memory: Vectorize Hindsight (Retain, Recall, Reflect)
• LLM: Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) / OpenAI
